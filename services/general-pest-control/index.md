## Keep Your Home Pest-Free Year Round

Iowa's seasons bring different pest pressures — ants in spring, spiders in fall, cockroaches and silverfish looking for warmth in winter. Our general pest control program covers the full cycle.

**Common pests we treat:**
- Ants (carpenter, odorous house, pavement)
- Spiders (including wolf spiders common in central Iowa)
- Cockroaches
- Earwigs and silverfish
- Stinging insects (wasps, yellow jackets)

## What's Included

Every general pest control visit includes an exterior perimeter treatment, interior spot treatment as needed, and a check of common entry points like door sweeps, gaps around pipes, and foundation cracks.

We use EPA-registered products that are safe for families and pets once dry.

## Treatment Schedule

Most homes benefit from quarterly visits. We offer one-time treatments as well, but a recurring plan gives you the best protection against re-infestation.

**Typical timeline:** Initial treatment takes 45–60 minutes. Results are usually visible within 24–48 hours.
