**What's included**

Every visit includes an exterior perimeter treatment, interior spot treatment as needed, and a check of common entry points like door sweeps, gaps around pipes, and foundation cracks. We use EPA-registered products that are safe for families and pets once dry.

**Treatment schedule**

Most homes benefit from quarterly visits. We offer one-time treatments as well, but a recurring plan gives you the best protection against re-infestation. Your technician will walk you through the right cadence for your home and pest pressure after the initial inspection.
