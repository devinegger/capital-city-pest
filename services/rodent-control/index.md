## Stop Mice and Rats Before They Cause Real Damage

Rodents aren't just a nuisance — they chew wiring, contaminate food, and can compromise the structural integrity of your home. Iowa's cold winters drive mice indoors starting in October, often through gaps no bigger than a dime.

## Our Approach

We don't just set traps and leave. Our rodent control process has three phases:

1. **Inspection** — We identify active entry points, nesting areas, and travel paths inside and outside your home.
2. **Trapping and removal** — We place tamper-resistant bait stations and snap traps in key locations and monitor them until activity stops.
3. **Exclusion** — We seal entry points with steel wool, caulk, and hardware cloth so the problem doesn't come back.

## When You Need This Service

- You've seen droppings in the kitchen, pantry, or garage
- You hear scratching in walls or ceilings at night
- You've found gnaw marks on food packaging or wiring
- You're preparing your home for winter and want to get ahead of it

**Typical timeline:** Most active infestations are resolved in 2–3 visits over 1–2 weeks. Exclusion work is scheduled after trapping is complete.
