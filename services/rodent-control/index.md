**Inspection**

We identify active entry points, nesting areas, and travel paths inside and outside your home. No two homes are the same — we map what we find before recommending a treatment plan.

**Trapping and removal**

We place tamper-resistant bait stations and snap traps in key locations and monitor them until activity stops. We don't just set traps and leave — we follow up until the problem is resolved.

**Exclusion**

We seal entry points with steel wool, caulk, and hardware cloth so the problem doesn't come back. Exclusion is what separates a permanent fix from a temporary patch.
