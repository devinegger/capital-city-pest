**Heat treatment**

We raise the temperature in the affected area to 120°F+ and hold it there long enough to kill bed bugs and their eggs at every life stage. Heat reaches inside walls, furniture, and mattresses where sprays can't penetrate. This is our most effective single-treatment option — no re-entry wait, no chemical residue.

**Chemical treatment**

A targeted application of residual insecticides and contact-kill products. Typically requires two visits 2 weeks apart to catch newly hatched eggs. A good option for single rooms or as a follow-up to heat treatment.

We provide a written preparation checklist before every appointment so you know exactly what to do to get the best results.
