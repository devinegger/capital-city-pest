## Bed Bugs Don't Go Away on Their Own

Bed bugs are among the hardest pests to eliminate without professional help. They hide in mattress seams, behind outlets, inside furniture joints, and in wall voids — and a single missed cluster can restart an infestation.

## Our Treatment Methods

**Heat Treatment**
We raise the temperature in the affected area to 120°F+ and hold it there long enough to kill bed bugs and their eggs at every life stage. Heat reaches inside walls, furniture, and mattresses where sprays can't penetrate. This is our most effective single-treatment option.

**Chemical Treatment**
A targeted application of residual insecticides and contact kill products. Typically requires two visits 2 weeks apart to catch newly hatched eggs. Good option for single rooms or as a follow-up to heat treatment.

## Signs You Have Bed Bugs

- Small rust-colored stains on sheets or mattress seams
- Itchy bites in a line or cluster, often on arms and shoulders
- Live bugs (apple-seed sized, flat, reddish-brown) in mattress folds
- Shed skins or tiny white eggs in furniture seams

**Typical timeline:** Heat treatment is a single-day process. Chemical treatment involves 2 visits over 3–4 weeks. We provide a written preparation checklist before every treatment.
