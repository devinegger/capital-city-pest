**Barrier spray**

We treat resting sites — shrubs, tall grass, shaded areas, fence lines — with a barrier spray that kills adult mosquitoes on contact and provides residual protection for 3–4 weeks. Re-treatment is included at no charge if you're not satisfied after your first visit.

**Program options**

- **Monthly treatments** — May through September, 5 visits
- **One-time event spray** — Great for cookouts, graduation parties, or weddings
- **Bi-monthly plan** — For lower-pressure yards or tighter budgets

Every visit includes a larvicide treatment for any standing water on your property (birdbaths, gutters, low spots) and written notes on conditions found and any drainage issues to address.
