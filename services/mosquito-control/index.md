## Take Back Your Backyard

Iowa summers mean outdoor gatherings, kids in the yard, and evenings on the patio — but mosquitoes can make all of that miserable. Our seasonal mosquito program reduces mosquito populations so you can actually use your outdoor spaces.

## How It Works

We treat resting sites — shrubs, tall grass, shaded areas, fence lines — with a barrier spray that kills adult mosquitoes on contact and provides residual protection for 3–4 weeks. We also identify and address standing water sources that serve as breeding sites.

**Program options:**
- **Monthly treatments** — May through September, 5 visits
- **One-time event spray** — Great for cookouts, graduation parties, or weddings
- **Bi-monthly plan** — For lower-pressure yards or tighter budgets

## What's Included

- Perimeter barrier spray targeting resting areas
- Larvicide treatment for any standing water on your property (birdbaths, gutters, low spots)
- Written notes on conditions found and any drainage issues to address

**Typical results:** Noticeable reduction in mosquito activity within 24 hours. Full effect peaks at 48–72 hours. Re-treatment is included at no charge if you're not satisfied after your first visit.
