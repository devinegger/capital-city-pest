## Local Expertise. Real Results.

Capitol City Pest Solutions was founded in Des Moines by a family that grew up here and wanted to build something they could be proud of. We started with a single truck and a commitment to honest pricing and thorough work — and we've grown by keeping that same standard every time we walk through a customer's door.

We're not a national franchise. Every technician on our team is Iowa-licensed, background-checked, and trained on the specific pest pressures that central Iowa homeowners and businesses face. We know what drives termite activity along the Des Moines River corridor, how bad the mosquito season gets in a wet June, and what it takes to keep rodents out of older homes with limestone foundations.

## Our Values

**Transparency** — We explain what we found, what we're treating, and why. No upsells you don't need.

**Safety** — We use EPA-registered products and follow label requirements to the letter. We'll tell you exactly what we applied and when it's safe to re-enter.

**Accountability** — If a treatment doesn't work, we come back. That's not a marketing line — it's how we've kept customers for over a decade.

## Our Team

Our crew of licensed technicians collectively holds certifications in general pest control, termite treatment, and wood-destroying organism inspection. We invest in continuing education every year because pest control science keeps improving and our customers deserve the benefit of it.
